# Mythological / devotional recipe (from Ep 1 and the 14 Nov tests)
- Open on the strongest face or hero image, no fade from black. One slow push-in (3-9 %) on the hook line.
- Deity entrance: Warm Spread-style bloom (ffmpeg warm-light intro) or fadewhite xfade. Once per video.
- Divine presence shot: corner light. CapCut Corner Sunflare if streaks matter; ffmpeg corner glow otherwise.
- Scene changes: 0.4-0.5 s dissolve. Hard cuts inside a scene. No glitch, shake or fire-style effects.
- Overlays (titles, end card) start on a cut or at least 0.5 s away from one.
- Sound: natural sounds from the clips + narration; no music in the file (songs are added in the Instagram app).
- Grade: `GRADE_SCALE=1080:1920 scripts/grade.sh in out 1.0 rose`. Captions: Telugu ASS, burned in.
- End card names the next episode.
