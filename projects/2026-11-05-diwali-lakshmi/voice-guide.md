# Voice recording guide — Diwali: why Lakshmi Devi comes home

**You don't need to match the video while recording.** Read the 7 lines in order with a
**2-second pause between lines**. Claude cuts each line and places it on its shot afterwards.

## Before you press record

- Quiet room, fan and AC off, phone on silent. Soft furnishings (curtains, bed, cupboard of
  clothes) cut echo; a bare room sounds hollow.
- Phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side (stops "p"/"b" pops).
- Say 3 seconds of nothing at the start: Claude uses it to remove background hiss.
- Any recorder app is fine (m4a, mp3 or wav). Keep the phone still while you speak.

## How to sound

Calm, warm, slow: like telling a story at a pooja, not like a news reader.
Smile slightly on the last line. Don't rush: the video has room for every line.

## The 7 lines (read in this order)

| # | Say this | Time on screen | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | దీపావళి రోజు మనం దీపాలు ఎందుకు వెలిగిస్తాం? | 0–3 s (her face) | A real question, gently curious. Rise on "వెలిగిస్తాం?" |
| 2 | క్షీరసాగర మథనం నుండి… | 3–8.5 s (ocean, lotus) | Slow, a little mysterious. Let "నుండి…" trail off |
| 3 | కమలంపై లక్ష్మీదేవి ఆవిర్భవించింది | 8.5–13.5 s (hero shot) | The big moment: fuller voice, with reverence |
| 4 | సిరిసంపదలను ప్రసాదించే తల్లి | 13.5–18 s (elephants, coins) | Warm, devotional |
| 5 | అమావాస్య చీకటిలో ఆమెను ఇంటికి ఆహ్వానించేందుకే… | 18–22.5 s (village, footprints) | Softer, building up. Trail off at "…ందుకే…" |
| 6 | …మనం దీపాలు వెలిగిస్తాం | 22.5–25.5 s (doorway) | The answer: settled and clear |
| 7 | దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు | 25.5–29 s (blessing) | Bright and warm, a smile in your voice |

Each line has 3–5 seconds of screen time, so a natural, unhurried pace fits.
If a line has longer screen time than you need, that's fine: silence over the visuals works.

## Takes

- Record **2–3 full takes** in one file or separate files. Say "take 2" before the second.
- If you stumble on a line, pause, and say that line again: Claude keeps the best one.
- Optional: one take reading while watching `final.mp4` play (to feel the rhythm).

## Send it

Put the file(s) in Google Drive (any folder) or send them to yourself on Telegram, then tell
Claude where they are. Claude will: trim, remove hiss, place each line on its shot, set YouTube
loudness (−14 LUFS), and make three versions: voice only, voice + soft music bed, and the
captioned version with voice.
