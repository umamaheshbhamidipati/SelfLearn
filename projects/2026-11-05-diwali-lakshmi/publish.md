## Hook check (before posting; the first AI reel lost viewers in 2 s)

- [ ] **First frame** is shot 01 (her face lit by diyas), with the question text already on screen
- [ ] Something moves or changes in the first 1–2 seconds (blink, flame flicker, push-in)
- [ ] **Cover** set to shot 04 (hero on the lotus) or 01 (face), not a dark frame
- [ ] Length ~28 s, no dead seconds at the end
- [ ] Audio: a devotional Lakshmi / Diwali track from Instagram's library (not a dance track)
- [ ] "AI content" label on (Instagram) / "altered or synthetic content" ticked (YouTube)
