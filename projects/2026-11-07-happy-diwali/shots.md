## Still prompts (Gemini)

01 — Thousands of glowing clay diyas floating up into the night sky from a small Telugu village with tiled roofs, forming a flowing river of golden light rising towards the stars, seen from the village street looking up.
02 — High aerial view at night of an Andhra Pradesh temple town: a tall lit temple gopuram in the centre, every rooftop and street lined with rows of glowing diyas, a few golden fireworks blooming in the sky.
03 — Close-up of a smiling elderly Telugu grandmother in a maroon silk saree with a small gold nose ring, her face lit warmly from below by a diya she holds, hands folded in namaste.
04 — Medium close-up of a young Telugu woman (adult, early twenties) in a green-and-gold silk saree lighting a clay diya with a matchstick on a doorstep, eyes shining, smiling at the camera.
05 — A cheerful Telugu man in his thirties in a white dhoti and kurta lighting a flower-pot firework on the front step of a village house; golden sparks fountain up, he laughs, leaning back.
06 — Extreme macro of a woman's hands with gold bangles placing a clay diya in the centre of a white muggulu rangoli pattern on the ground; the flame has just caught.
07 — An adult Telugu couple on a moonlit terrace, seen from slightly behind and to the side, looking up at golden fireworks, warm light on their faces, her head on his shoulder.
08 — Wide shot of a stone ghat on the Godavari river at night, hundreds of small diyas floating on the water, a lit temple on the bank, silhouettes of people releasing more diyas.
09 — Huge golden fireworks bursting over a dark village skyline with palm trees and a temple tower, long trails of light, reflections on wet roofs.
10 — (in the Lakshmi chat, master image as reference) Goddess Lakshmi appearing large and luminous in the night sky above a lamp-lit Telugu village, blessing with her lower right hand, lotuses in her upper hands, golden light rays falling on the rooftops.
