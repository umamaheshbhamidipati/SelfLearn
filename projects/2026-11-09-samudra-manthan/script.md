# Script — Samudra Manthan (సముద్ర మథనం) — 3-episode series

Owner decisions (2026-11-10): **3 episodes**, theme line OK; Parvati and Rahu left to Claude:
- **Parvati: included, in two steps.** (1) She consents before Shiva drinks: in the text (Bhagavata 8.7.41, Bhavani
  consents). (2) Her hand on his throat: narrated as **భక్తుల విశ్వాసం** (devotees' belief), not as scripture.
- **Rahu: dropped** from Ep 3 (too much in 75 s; Ketu is later tradition). Possible stand-alone Short later
  (Rahu and the eclipse).

**Theme (every episode lands on it):** అమృతం ముందు విషం వస్తుంది — దాన్ని ఎవరో ఒకరు భరించాలి.
("Poison comes before nectar; someone has to bear it.")

**Format:** 9:16, 60–75 s each. Instagram first, then YouTube Short. Playlist "సముద్ర మథనం", title prefix
"సముద్ర మథనం | Ep N". 9:16 because Instagram is the primary platform; Veo clips come in one aspect ratio, so a
16:9 full film would need clips generated again. Instead, join the three episodes later into one vertical
video (no new credits).

**Production order:** Ep 2 first (hero episode, stands alone, best test of Shiva/Parvati consistency), then Ep 1,
then Ep 3. Ep 2 opens with a 1-line recap so it works without Ep 1.
