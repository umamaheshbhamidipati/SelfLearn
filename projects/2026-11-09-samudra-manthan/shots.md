**Parvati block:** Goddess Parvati, divinely beautiful young woman (about 23), flawless crisp luminous skin with

otherworldly celestial jewellery: a crown of woven light and translucent crystal, ornaments of glowing celestial
metal, small pieces floating weightlessly. Two arms. Dignified, regal.

**Indra block (male Deva lead):** Indra, king of the Devas, handsome adult man (about 30), unmistakably masculine,
strong square jaw, defined brow, short neat black beard and moustache, broad shoulders, bare muscular chest, hair
tied up under a tall golden crown of light, ivory and gold silk dhoti, golden vajra in one hand. Never "beautiful,
graceful, soft".

**Deva group block:** exactly [N] adult men, no women, Devas in ivory, cream and gold silk dhotis with gold
armlets, bare muscular backs, hair tied up under small gold crowns, seen from behind or in profile, faces small or
turned away. Always screen-right.

**Asura group block:** exactly [N] powerful adult men, no women, Asuras in maroon, bronze and black-gold armour,

**Setting words:** the Ksheera Sagara, a vast ocean of milk-white water and cream foam (not blue sea); Mandara, a
forested mountain standing in the ocean, resting on the shell of the giant tortoise Kurma; Vasuki, one many-hooded
serpent king wound once around the mountain, one continuous body, Asuras holding the head side, Devas the tail side.
