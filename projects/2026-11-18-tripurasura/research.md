## Iconography (our choices, art convention)
- Gold city top (heaven, cloud tops at dawn light), silver city middle (sky, moonlit), iron city low (over the
  earth, dark blue-grey steel, smoke-free). Each brother colour-coded to his city: gold / silver / steel-blue.
- Asuras: young men, small bronze crowns, not monstrous (Samudra Manthan rule).
- Brahma: four faces, young (CLAUDE.md casting), on a lotus of light; no grey beard.
- Shiva in Ep 1 only as a teaser silhouette (no face): crescent, matted hair, trident on a snow peak.
- No gore: the burning in Ep 3 is light and ash, no bodies.
