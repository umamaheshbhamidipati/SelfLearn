# Script — త్రిపురాసుర సంహారం (3 episodes; plan 18 Nov 2026. Ep 1 narration APPROVED 18 Nov; owner decisions: preacher left out, arrow only (smile tradition in Ep 3's caption only), schedule Ep 1 Fri 20, Ep 2 Sun 22, Ep 3 Tue 24 Nov morning)

## Decisions for the owner (proposed, not decided)
1. **Leave out the "preacher" episode** (Vishnu sends a figure who turns the Asuras away from the Vedas,
   SP 3.45-48). It can read as mocking another living religion and is not needed for a 3-part Short. The gap it
   leaves (in SP they were Shiva's devotees, 3.6-7) is bridged by **pride**: "వరగర్వంతో ముల్లోకాలనూ వేధించారు"
   (the gods' own complaint, SP 2.59-60). **Recommend: leave out.**
2. **The smile twist.** "మునుగోడు కోపాన్ని" in the pasted text is garbled. The smile version (the cities burn from
   Shiva's smile, the arrow a formality) is **Tamil Shaiva tradition** (Tevaram), **not** in the Shiva Purana,
   where one arrow burns them (10.25-27). Options: (a) arrow only (recommended); (b) arrow in the narration, the
   smile in the caption as "some tellings"; (c) one Ep 3 line "తమిళ శైవ సంప్రదాయంలో… ఆయన చిరునవ్వుకే పురాలు
   భస్మమయ్యాయని చెబుతారు" (+3 s).
3. **The third city = iron** (gold / silver / iron = heaven / sky / earth). SP 1.47 says steel; the "రాగి"
   (copper) in the pasted text comes from a translator's footnote that contradicts the verse.
4. **Karthika Purnima = Tue 24 Nov 2026** (publicholidays.in, hindutone/Drik, dekhopanchang; tithi 23 Nov 11:42 PM
   to 24 Nov 8:23 PM IST). Ep 3 on 24 Nov morning is feasible only if Ep 1 is approved by 19 Nov. Fallback: a
   stand-alone deepam Short on 24 Nov.
5. **Ep 2 "the Earth is the chariot":** SP 8.6 says a golden chariot (sun and moon the wheels). Earth-as-chariot is
   the popular / Mahabharata image. Use "భూమే రథం" framed as a telling, or SP's "బంగారు రథం"? (Ep 2, later.)

## Ep 2 — దివ్య రథం (The divine chariot): build-up montage, ends on Shiva drawing the bow (~35 s)
Structure: no rewind; each line adds one part of the chariot, push-ins cut on the beat, one hero clip at the end.
Draft lines (to be written and audited after Ep 1 is approved):
1. Hook: the gods (Devas) beg Shiva, "only you can do it" (SP 2-3: first Brahma, then Shiva).
2. The chariot: the Earth / a golden chariot (decision 5); the Sun and the Moon its wheels.
3. The four Vedas its horses; Brahma the charioteer.
4. Mount Meru the bow; Adishesha the bowstring.
5. And the arrow… Vishnu himself; Agni its tip.
6. Twist (SP 9.8-9): the chariot sinks under Shiva's weight; Vishnu as a bull holds it up (optional, +4 s).
7. Shiva takes the bow and waits for the moment. Teaser: "వెయ్యేళ్ల క్షణం… వస్తోంది. తర్వాతి భాగంలో."
Clips (2): the chariot rolls forward with sun/moon wheels turning; Shiva draws the bow (no name in the prompt).

## Ep 3 — ఒక్క బాణం · కార్తీక దీపం (One arrow, the Karthika lamp): countdown, ends on a call to light a lamp (~38 s)
Structure: present-tense countdown, then the festival tie-in and a direct address to the viewer.
1. Hook: "వెయ్యేళ్ల క్షణం వచ్చింది." The cities slide into line.
2. Shiva waits; a voice: first worship Ganesha (SP 10.5-9). He does; then he sees the cities joined.
3. One arrow, bright as countless suns (10.25): the three burn to ash in an instant (light and ash, no bodies).
4. Only Maya, the devotee, was spared (10.38-39) (optional).
5. (Optional, decision 2: the smile line.)
6. Tie-in, framed as belief: "అందుకే కార్తీక పౌర్ణమి నాడు దీపాలు వెలిగిస్తాం… జ్వాలా తోరణం… అని నమ్మకం."
7. CTA: "ఈ కార్తీక పౌర్ణమి, మీ ఇంటి గుమ్మంలో ఒక దీపం వెలిగించండి. ఓం నమః శివాయ." (Shiva looks at the viewer:
   preflight "eyes" rule; a doorstep lamp under the line: "narration vs picture" rule.)
Clips (2-3): the arrow leaves the bow; the three cities burn to light; (reuse) diyas from
`projects/2026-11-08-karthika-deepam` stills (0 credits, our own).
