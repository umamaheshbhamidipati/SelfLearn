# Ep 2 + Ep 3 narration audit (18 Nov 2026; narration APPROVED 18 Nov with Ep 3 L3 "పూజించండి"; decision 5 = SP golden chariot)

SP = Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Yuddha Khanda (Shastri translation; ch 8-10 read in full).
OK / FLAG (owner decides). Length estimate: 9 lines each, about 32-36 s of speech (Ep 1's 10 lines ≈ 36 s).

## Decision 5 (the Earth as the chariot): recommendation
SP ch 8 never names the Earth: the chariot is "golden in colour" (8.6), with the sun and moon as wheels. The
Earth-as-chariot image is the popular / Mahabharata telling (not checked here). **Recommend SP's "బంగారు దివ్య
రథం"**: nothing in it needs a "some tellings" frame, and the hook line (L1, "the whole universe became a chariot")
already gives the cosmic scale. Alternative L3: "కొన్ని గాథల ప్రకారం భూమే రథం. సూర్యచంద్రులే దాని చక్రాలు." (+1 s).
