## What a credit buys (measured)

- Veo 3.1 Lite: **10 credits per 8 s clip**, about 1.5–2 tries per usable clip → **~15–20 credits per usable clip**.
- One 30–35 s episode: 5–8 Veo clips plus free still push-ins → **50–100 credits** (Ep 1 used 50; Ep 2+3 together 70).
- Stills (Nano Banana in Flow or the Gemini app): **0 credits**.
- Seedance 2.5 on Higgsfield: 100 credits ≈ **7 clips** (Higgsfield's figure; cost per clip unconfirmed until the first test).

## The rule: spend the credits that expire first

1. **Daily 50 first.** They are gone tomorrow. 50 = 5 Veo clips = most of one episode.
2. **Monthly 200 is the reserve:** re-rolls, hero shots, a 1080p upgrade if the owner approves one.
   Spend what's left in the last week before the 15th.
3. **Higgsfield 100 only for shots Veo can't do** (see the list below). Spend what's left before the 25th.

Credits are not the limit. The owner's time and Claude's usage limit are. 1,700 Flow credits a month would make
15+ episodes; we post 1–2 a week. So **don't spend credits to avoid waste on clips nobody will post.** Unused
daily credits are fine.

## Higgsfield 100/month: where it goes

Use it on the shot types Veo has failed on (`docs/lessons.md`):

- hands holding or passing an object (diya plate, hand-offs: 10–11 Nov);
- long creatures or ropes with two ends (Vasuki: 13 Nov);
- an object changing state (a diya lifted onto water);
- the same face across several clips (Seedance takes up to 30 reference images).

Monthly split: **month 1 (25 Nov–25 Dec):** the Seedance-vs-Veo test (`docs/experiments.md`): 2–3 clips of one
hard shot in 480p draft, then 1 final. Keep ~40 in reserve. **After that:** 3–5 hard shots a month, only if the
test wins on cost per usable clip; otherwise use it for still push-ins in Veo and cancel the renewal before
2027-11-25.

Blocker: the connector has no generate tool yet (25 Nov). Until it does, the owner generates on higgsfield.ai by
hand from prompts I write. No browser automation there (their fair-use rule).
