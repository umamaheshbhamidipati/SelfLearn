# Weekly rhythm (owner has a full-time job; set 12 Nov 2026)

**Goal: a steady rhythm, not daily videos.** Daily video generation is out of reach (about 400 Flow credits a week = 1-2 videos) and near-identical daily clips risk YouTube's inauthentic-content rule.

- **Daily (10-15 min):** one small step. Log it: `scripts/streak.sh add "what I did" 10`. Check: `scripts/streak.sh show`.
  Streak rule: one missed day is fine, two in a row ends it. Then restart with one small step.
- **Weekly:** one finished post (20 s explainer one week, a Samudra Manthan episode the next). Claude pre-screens with `docs/preflight-checklist.md`; the owner approves a PASS/FAIL summary (about 5 min).
- **Monthly:** one light week with no new video (rest), and a look at `docs/metrics.csv`.
- **Count inputs, not only views:** posts finished, hooks tested, lessons logged. Milestones: 25, 100, 500 subscribers; ignore income until the platform thresholds are close.
- **Small steps that count:** read yesterday's numbers; approve a still; record one voiceover line; write the next hook; log a lesson.
