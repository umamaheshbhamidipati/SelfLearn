## Card 2: Vasuki

- End line: "వాసుకి… క్షీరసాగర మథనంలో తాడు." + save line.

## Card 3: Chandra on Shiva's head
- Hook (0:00): "శివుని తలపై చంద్రుడు ఎందుకు ఉంటాడు?"
- Facts (Puranic account, say "పురాణ కథనం ప్రకారం"): Daksha cursed Chandra to waste away; Chandra prayed to Shiva;
  Shiva placed him on his head, so the moon wanes and grows again. Hence the name Chandrashekhara.
- Iconography to confirm: crescent in the hair as in our Shiva reference; no Daksha shown.
- Pictures: Shiva reference still (push-in on the crescent) + Neelakantha portrait still. 0 credits.
- End line: "అందుకే శివుడు… చంద్రశేఖరుడు." + save line.

## Card 4: Panchatantra - the lion and the clever rabbit
- Hook (0:00): "సింహాన్ని ఓడించింది… ఒక చిన్న కుందేలు!"
- Story: a cruel lion demands one animal a day. The rabbit arrives late and says another lion stopped him. He leads
  the lion to a well; the lion sees his own reflection, jumps in to fight it, and is gone.
- Pictures: lion roaring in the forest; small rabbit before the lion; lion looking into the well at his reflection
  (hero shot, 1 clip); animals free at dawn.
- Moral line: "బలం కన్నా… బుద్ధి గొప్పది."

## Card 5: Tenali Rama - the thieves and the well
- Hook (0:00): "దొంగలతోనే తన తోటకు నీళ్లు పెట్టించాడు!"
- Story: Tenali Rama notices thieves hiding in his garden. He loudly tells his wife their valuables are in a chest
  and drops it into the well. All night the thieves draw water to reach it, watering his dry garden. At dawn he
  thanks them and they run.
- Pictures: Rama and his wife on the veranda at night; chest splashing into the well; thieves drawing water by
  moonlight (1 clip); Rama smiling at the green garden at sunrise.
- Moral line: "తెలివి ఉంటే… కష్టం కూడా లాభమే."
