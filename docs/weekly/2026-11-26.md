## Story: Gajendra Moksham (గజేంద్ర మోక్షం), new 3-episode series

Why this one: Telugu families know it from Pothana's Bhagavatam (the poems are 15th-century, public domain); it uses
our locked Vishnu (`vishnu_C`), so faces stay consistent; it stays in Karthika month and leads into Vaikuntha
Ekadashi (date NOT verified: check a panchangam before planning that video). It also has the perfect hard shot
for the Seedance vs Veo test (shot 1-01). Story details are checked against the Bhagavatam (8th skandha) when the
script is written, not from memory.

- **Ep 1 "పట్టు" (The grip):** the elephant king at the lake, the crocodile seizes his leg, the long struggle, his strength fails. Ends as he raises a lotus.
- **Ep 2 "శరణు" (Surrender):** his prayer, Vishnu hears it in Vaikuntha and rushes out on Garuda without a word to Lakshmi. Ends as the chakra leaves his hand.
- **Ep 3 "మోక్షం" (Release), next week:** the chakra frees him, the crocodile becomes a Gandharva again, Gajendra is liberated.

Each episode is ~35 s, ~9 narration lines, the same format as Tripurasura. Animals plus young-adult deities only.
